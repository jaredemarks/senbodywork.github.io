Trained in Chiang Mai

A sixty-hour certification in traditional Thai yoga massage, studied in Chiang Mai, Thailand — the practice's own home. The training is hands-on and immersive: less a technique to perform, more a way of working with the body that's been refined over centuries.

"Sen" refers to the energy lines traditional Thai massage works along — the body's own map, followed rather than imposed on.

What a session involves

Ninety minutes

The traditional length for this work — slow and thorough rather than rushed. Sessions are held wherever you are, mat provided.

Fully clothed

Worked on a floor mat, in loose comfortable clothing. No oils, no massage table required.

Stretch and pressure

A rhythmic mix of assisted stretching, acupressure, and yoga-based movement — often described as feeling like yoga done to you.
